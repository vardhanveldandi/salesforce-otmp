# REPORTS

# Overview

The Online Training Management Platform (OTMP) uses Salesforce Reports to monitor students, enrollments, payments, trainers, and overall business performance.

# OTMP Enrollment Report

## Purpose

Provides an overview of student enrollments, course registrations, and payment status.

## Information Included

Student Name, Course Name, Enrollment Status, Payment Status, Enrollment Date and Record Count.

## Business Benefits

- Tracks student enrollments.
- Monitors enrollment and payment status.
- Helps manage course registrations.
- Supports quick reporting and decision-making.
- Improves enrollment management.

# Payment Revenue Report

## Purpose

Provides an overview of student payments, revenue, and pending balances.

## Information Included

 Student Name, Payment Status, Payment Amount, Pending Amount, Payment Date, Course Name, Batch ID and Total Revenue.
 
## Business Benefits

- Tracks student payments.
- Monitors revenue and pending balances.
- Supports financial reporting.
- Helps identify pending payments.
- Improves payment management.

# OTMP AI Analytics Report

This report includes Most Asked Queries Report, Agent Usage Report , Average Satisfaction Score Report, Average Response Time Report and AI Interaction Status Report(Success Vs Failed Vs Pending).

## 1. Most Asked Queries Report

## Purpose

Provides an overview of the most frequently asked student queries handled by the AI Support Agent.

## Information Included

Query Type, AI Interaction Name , Number of Queries and  Record Count

## Business Benefits

- Identifies common student queries.
- Helps improve AI responses.
- Supports better student support.
- Provides insights into query trends.
- Improves overall service efficiency.

## 2. Agent Usage Report

## Purpose

Provides an overview of AI agent usage and the types of queries handled.

## Information Included

Agent Name , Query Type , AI Interaction Name, Number of Interactions and Record Count

## Business Benefits

- Tracks AI agent usage.
- Monitors query distribution.
- Measures agent performance.
- Supports AI performance analysis.
- Improves support efficiency.

## 3. Average Satisfaction Score Report

## Purpose

Provides an overview of student satisfaction with AI interactions and support services.

## Information Included

AI Interaction Name, Satisfaction Score, Average Satisfaction Score and Total Records.

## Business Benefits

- Measures student satisfaction.
- Monitors AI support quality.
- Identifies areas for improvement.
- Supports service quality analysis.
- Enhances the overall student experience.

## 4. Average Response Time Report

## Purpose

Provides an overview of the average response time of AI interactions.

## Information Included

AI Interaction Name, Response Time, Average Response Time and Total Records.

## Business Benefits

- Monitors AI response speed.
- Measures support efficiency.
- Identifies response delays.
- Supports performance improvement.
- Enhances the student support experience.

## 5. AI Interaction Status Report(Success Vs Failed Vs Pending)

## Purpose

Track the status of AI interactions and monitor whether requests are successful, pending, or failed.

## Information Included

AI Interaction Name, Interaction Status (Success, Pending, Failed), Total AI Interactions and Record Count by Status.

## Business Benefit

- Monitors AI interaction performance.
- Identifies failed interactions for troubleshooting.
- Tracks pending requests requiring attention.
- Measures overall AI service success rate.
- Helps improve AI reliability and user experience.

# DASHBOARDS

# Overview

The OTMP project includes interactive Salesforce dashboards that provide real-time insights into payments, enrollments, trainer performance, and AI interactions. These dashboards help administrators monitor business performance and make informed decisions.

# DashBoard Summary

-  OTMP Payment Revenue Dashboard
  Summary: Provides a complete view of payment collections, revenue trends, pending payments, and batch-wise revenue to monitor the organization's financial 
           Performance.
  
-  OTMP Enrollment Dashboard
  Summary: Provides insights into student enrollments, course popularity, enrollment status, and learning trends to effectively monitor training operations.
  
-  OTMP AI Analytics Dashboard
  Summary: Monitors Agentforce AI performance by tracking student queries, agent usage, response time, interaction success rate, and student satisfaction.

-  Executive OTMP Dashboard
  Summary: Provides a unified view of OTMP business performance by combining enrollment, revenue, payment, course, and AI analytics into a single executive
           dashboard for informed decision-making.
           
# OTMP Payment - Revenue Dashboard

## Overview

This Dashboard provides a complete view of payment collections and revenue performance in the Online Training Management Platform (OTMP). It helps administrators monitor revenue, payment status, and monthly collection trends.

## Key Metrics

Total Payment Revenue, Payment Collection Status (Pending & Completed), Monthly Revenue Collection, Batch-wise Revenue, Payment Revenue Funnel, Payment Revenue Details

## Business Benefits

- Tracks total revenue collected.
- Monitors pending and completed payments.
- Identifies monthly revenue trends.
- Compares revenue across batches.
- Supports financial reporting and business decisions.

# OTMP Enrollment Dashboard

## Overview

This Dashboard provides a complete view of student enrollments across courses. It helps administrators monitor enrollment status, course popularity, and overall enrollment performance.

## Key Metrics

Total Enrollments, Enrolled vs Completed Status, Top Courses by Student Count, Course-wise Enrollment Distribution, Enrollment Report Details, Enrollment Funnel Analysis

## Business Benefits

- Tracks overall student enrollments.
- Monitors enrollment and completion status.
- Identifies the most popular courses.
- Supports course planning and batch management.
- Helps improve enrollment monitoring and decision-making.

# OTMP AI Analytics Dashboard

## Overview

The OTMP AI Analytics Dashboard provides a real-time view of the performance of the OTMP Student Support Agent (Agentforce). It tracks student queries, AI agent usage, response quality, satisfaction scores, and interaction success to measure the effectiveness of AI-powered support.

## Key Metrics

- Most Asked Student Queries – Shows the most common query types (Enrollment, Payment, Batch, Trainer).
- Agent Usage – Displays how frequently the AI agent handles different query categories.
- Average Student Satisfaction Score – Measures user satisfaction with AI responses.
- Success vs Failed Interactions – Compares successful AI responses with failed interactions.
- Average Response Time – Shows how quickly the AI agent responds to student queries.
- AI Performance Summary – Provides an overall snapshot of Agentforce performance.

## Business Benefits

- Improves the quality of AI-powered student support.
- Identifies the most common student queries.
- Monitors AI agent performance and usage.
- Measures student satisfaction with AI responses.
- Tracks successful and failed interactions for continuous improvement.
- Helps administrators optimize Agentforce efficiency and response times.

# Executive OTMP Dashboard 

## Overview

The Executive OTMP Dashboard provides a consolidated view of the overall performance of the Online Training Management Platform (OTMP). It combines enrollment, revenue, batch performance, payment collection, and AI analytics into a single dashboard, enabling management to monitor key business metrics and make informed decisions.

## Key Metrics

- Total Enrollments – Displays the total number of student enrollments.
- Total Revenue – Shows the total payment revenue collected.
- Enrollment Status – Compares enrolled and completed students.
- Payment Collection Status – Displays completed and pending payments.
- Monthly Collections – Tracks monthly revenue trends.
- Batch-Wise Revenue – Shows revenue generated by each batch.
- Top Courses by Student Count – Identifies the most popular courses.
- Most Asked Student Queries – Displays common AI support requests.
- Agent Usage – Monitors Agentforce usage across query types.
- Success vs Failed Interactions – Compares successful and failed AI interactions.
- Average Response Time – Measures AI response speed.
- Average Student Satisfaction Score – Displays overall AI satisfaction rating.

## Business Benefits

- Provides a complete business performance overview in one place.
- Helps management monitor enrollments and revenue.
- Tracks payment collection and batch performance.
- Identifies popular courses and student demand.
- Measures AI agent performance and student satisfaction.
- Supports faster, data-driven business decisions.
- Enables continuous improvement of training operations and AI support services.
