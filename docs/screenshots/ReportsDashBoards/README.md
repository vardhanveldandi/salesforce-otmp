# OTMP Reports

This folder contains screenshots of the reports created for the Online Training Management Platform (OTMP) in Salesforce.

## Reports

* **Payment Revenue Report** – Tracks payment and revenue information.
* **OTMP Enrollment Report** – Provides student enrollment details.
* **Agent Usage Report** – Tracks Agentforce/AI agent usage.
* **Average Satisfaction Score Report** – Shows average student satisfaction.
* **Average Response Time Report** – Measures support response time.
* **AI Interaction Status Report** – Shows successful, failed, and pending AI interactions.
* **Most Asked Queries Report** – Identifies frequently asked student queries.


These reports provide operational, student-support, payment, enrollment, and **Agentforce AI analytics** for the OTMP platform.
